On October 3, 2025, our lab proudly celebrated the graduation of several members from Chulalongkorn University. <br>
<br>
We congratulate our graduates on this important milestone and wish them continued success in their future academic and professional careers. <br>
<br> The graduating members include Master's students Chayaporn Lakmuang (Bioinformatics) and Teeratat Kaewjon (Microbiology and Microbial Technology); undergraduate students in Microbiology Pichsinee Thaitawee (Peach) and Pran Panyakong; and BBTech students Nunchima Chantanasevee (Lukmai), Natsima Vichienchai (Patty), and Alisara Kaewplung (Fai).
