## SEA-ARMi 1st Online Training 
The **Southeast Asia Antifungal Resistance Monitoring Initiative (SEA-ARMi)** successfully launched its first online training session on **metagenomics preprocessing**, focusing on data curation and primary data analysis. 
{%
  include figure.html
  image="images/blog/Training2026.jpg"
%}
Metagenomics preprocessing is a critical step in transforming raw sequencing data into high-quality datasets suitable for downstream analyses. The training covered essential quality control procedures, data curation strategies, and computational workflows designed to minimize technical noise while preserving biologically meaningful information. 
<br> Participants were introduced to an integrated workflow for analyzing **WHO Fungal Priority Pathogens (WHO FPPL)** and **antimicrobial resistance (AMR) genes**. The tutorial assumes that raw sequencing reads have already been processed into count tables and focuses on the subsequent bioinformatics analyses. 
The training materials include: <br>
- Pipeline overview <br> 
- High-Performance Computing Cluster (HPCC) setup  <br>
- Shell scripting - R-based data analysis and visualization  <br>
- Detailed step-by-step scripts and documentation <br>
Training resources are available at: <br>
