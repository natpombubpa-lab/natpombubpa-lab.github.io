## First PhD Defense of NatPombubpa Lab 
On **9 June 2026**, **Niar Ibrahim**, the first Ph.D. student of the NatPombubpa Lab, successfully defended her doctoral dissertation after a three-hour oral examination.
{%
  include figure.html
  image="images/blog/Defense1.JPG"
%}
This milestone marks the first Ph.D. graduation from our research group and reflects years of dedication, perseverance, and scientific achievement in environmental microbiology and medical mycology. <br>
At the end of June, the lab celebrated this memorable achievement together with **Dr. Nuttapon Pombubpa**, **Dr. Ariya Chindamporn**, and **Dr. Sita Virakul**, recognizing the successful completion of this important academic journey.
